## Primary linkable resource

- Research URL: https://pdfenrich.com/research/pdf-email-attachment-size-study
- Dataset CSV: https://pdfenrich.com/research/attachment-size/pdf-attachment-size-study.csv
- Method and results JSON: https://pdfenrich.com/research/attachment-size/pdf-attachment-size-study.json
- Supporting tool: https://pdfenrich.com/compress-pdf

The study measures the raw and RFC 4648 Base64-encoded sizes of seven public PDF fixtures. It publishes byte counts, observed overhead, page counts, limitations, source fixtures, and machine-readable results. It is a suitable citation for articles explaining why an email attachment consumes more message space than its folder size.

## Outreach quality rules

1. Contact only pages where the dataset or product directly improves the reader's answer.
2. Personalize the opening sentence and name the exact section the research supports.
3. Request a citation only when the published data is relevant; do not demand specific anchor text.
4. Do not buy links, automate mass email, trade links, or submit to unrelated generic directories.
5. Track the target, contact date, response, earned URL, and the landing page receiving the link.
6. Recheck every live listing after product, pricing, privacy, or feature changes.
